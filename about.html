About Surgeo

Practical expertise for the paperwork behind mining.

Surgeo Services is a Nagpur-based mining consultancy focused on surveys, exploration support, technical documentation, applications and the day-to-day paperwork required across mining projects.

Our purpose

Bring field understanding and documentation together.

Mining work is not only about what happens at a site. It also depends on accurate surveys, properly prepared forms, technical reports, records and timely submissions. Surgeo Services is built around that practical reality.

We work as a support partner for clients who need help translating field information and project requirements into organized, usable documentation. The goal is simple: reduce administrative friction and help projects move forward with clearer information.
